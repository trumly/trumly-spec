# Code of conduct

Be kind and be specific. We want issues and discussions here to be useful to the people reading them.

- Treat people with respect. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic and assume good faith.
- Do not post other people's private information.

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, and lock or close threads, that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, email hello@trumly.co. For security problems, see `SECURITY.md` instead.
